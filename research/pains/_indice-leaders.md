# Pain index — kingdom & alliance leadership (D-0500 … D-0527)

Wave 1 collected 2026-10-13 (26 pains). **Wave 2 re-verified by Sub-admin REINO (2026-10-13):**
- every quote re-checked verbatim against the Arctic Shift archive and live pages;
- frequencies recounted for author independence;
- official patch notes 1.0.74–1.1.09 checked for features that absorb a pain;
- gaps filled for leader burnout, timezone and language.

Result: **28 pains — 15 `validada`, 12 `bruto`, 1 `descartada`.** Report: `research/facts/_verificacao-reino-wave2.md`.

**Scoring.** Score = frequency × gravity weight (alta 3, media 2, baixa 1). Frequency = independent reports or artefacts found, not prevalence. `validada` needs freq ≥ 5 in ≥ 2 channels.

Raw notes: `research/raw/pains-leaders/` (sources PLxx in `_sources.md`; workflows in `context/kingdom-admin-workflows.md`; interview guide in `_interview-guide.md`).

**Read with care (sample bias):**
- Reddit is reached only through the Arctic Shift archive and is member-heavy: often kicked or zeroed players. Leaders are under-represented.
- Some anti-"toxic leadership" posts are recruitment pitches.
- One cited author (Fantastic-Body6519) is a templated multi-subreddit persona account. Their quotes were removed from D-0500 and D-0519.
- Full-text archive search times out for rare terms, so title search was used for the gap topics. "NAP" stayed unreachable.

## Themes (updated)

1. **The DKP machine** (D-0500, D-0501, D-0502, D-0503, D-0504, D-0522).
   - Scan → DKP → requirements → reward/punish. Still entirely outside the game: no official export found.
   - The official **ROK Toolkit** (B08-0007) is the unknown that could change this.
2. **Legitimacy and fairness** (D-0506, D-0505, D-0525, D-0507, D-0524). Defending decisions matters more than computing them. "Fair/transparent MGE/DKP" is the top recruiting promise.
3. **Cohesion and conflict** (D-0513, D-0521, D-0514).
   - Civil war is the #1 reported kingdom-killer.
   - Official SoC tools (votes, coalitions, territory protection, Camp Treaty) address cross-kingdom and SoC cases. Nothing addresses pre-SoC intra-kingdom agreements.
4. **People flow** (D-0507, D-0508, D-0509, D-0510, D-0511, D-0520). Churn plus frequent rule changes (age limits tightened Mar 2026).
5. **Coordination and comms** (D-0517, D-0518, D-0515, D-0512, D-0526, D-0527, D-0516). Partly absorbed:
   - Bugle pins, voice chat, markers, Quick Checks, Army Groups
   - non-friend DM block (helps against impersonation scams)
6. **Leader load** (D-0519, now validated). Leadership is described as an unpaid second job. Lilith added "Alliance Leadership" achievements (Jan 2026).
