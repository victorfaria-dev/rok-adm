## Themes

1. **The DKP machine (D-0500, D-0501, D-0502, D-0503, D-0504, D-0522).** The kingdom's central admin loop is: scan → compute DKP → set/judge requirements → reward/punish. Every step is DIY: no official stats export, no standard formula, gameable data, fragile scanners. Leaders "turn into accountants". This is the most *tool-addressable* cluster and already a crowded paid market (ROKSTATS, Hero Scrolls, Botals, ROK Steward, APO).
2. **Legitimacy and fairness (D-0506, D-0505, D-0525, D-0507, D-0524).** The pain is less "computing" than *defending* decisions: who gets MGE, who is cut after KvK, who enters the Lost Kingdom. Members call it hoarding, dictatorship, abuse; leaders call breakers defectors. Transparency ("fair/transparent DKP", "fair MGE") is the #1 recruiting promise.
3. **Kingdom cohesion and conflict (D-0513, D-0521, D-0514).** Civil war is the single most reported kingdom-killer; rival leaderships, coercion of small alliances and insider sabotage via officer permissions. No mechanism records agreements (territory, zone rewards, MGE) between alliances.
4. **People flow (D-0507, D-0508, D-0509, D-0510, D-0511, D-0520).** Kingdoms constantly churn: recruit role holders, vet migrants, purge deadweight, protect a target seed, while Lilith keeps changing migration rules.
5. **Coordination & comms (D-0516, D-0517, D-0518, D-0515, D-0512).** KvK timelines per story, AoO/role signups, rules that only exist in chat, and impersonation scams. Mostly solved with Discord bots + Google Sheets.
6. **Leader load (D-0519).** Under everything: leadership is a second job; tool authors quit; R4s stay out of guilt.

## Surprises (verify further)

- **Seed manipulation by zeroing your own players** (D-0520): leaderships zero members to push kingdom power down into seed B; migrants are refused as "deadweight power". Seed math (top-300 tiered power, C04-0020) drives purges and intake.
- **Lilith is absorbing admin features fast:** battle roster + Watchlist (Oct 2025), officer permission categories + shared king skills (Jul 2025), Alliance Notes + Quick Checks (May 2026), Army Groups (Dec 2025), Acclaim contribution metric (Nov 2025), Conduct Score vs scripts (May 2026). Anything purely in-game is at risk of being absorbed (cf. C01-0025 title system).
- **Leader impersonation scams** (D-0515) targeting new players — 4 posts in 16 months.
- **"Kingdom for sale" / "Kingdom price (king)"** posts (2025) hint at trading kingship — single weak signal.
- **DKP as currency:** auctions of MGE ranks and gold-head events, fines paid to fighters, CP expiry.
- **Zeroing is the de-facto legal system**: 18 of 50 recent "zeroed" posts describe it as kingdom enforcement, not war.
