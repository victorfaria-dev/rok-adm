## Community guide on bans (T3)
- CS06 https://riseofkingdomshandbook.com/guides/riseofkingdoms-how-to-play-on-pc — "Published: August 18, 2026".
  - "Third-party automation is against the terms of service and detection has improved a lot."
  - "Bans come from automation and modified clients, not from the screen you play on."
  - "Emulators are fine; modified clients and bots are how accounts get banned."
  - multi-instance: "running two accounts is normal, but automating them is not."
  - mod apks: "These are either malware or a ban. Both, usually."

## Multiple characters / farm accounts
- CS47 https://www.appgamer.com/rise-of-kingdoms/strategy-guide/creating-a-farm-account (snippet): game lets you "start a number of other accounts in the game"; "usually allowed to only open two accounts per kingdom"; via Settings > Character Management. Undated fan site; not verified in-game. Note: ToS 17.1.16 forbids creating a Lilith Account "for anyone other than yourself" — farms for oneself are not addressed as forbidden; account *sharing* is (17.1.21).

## Fan sites / trademark

- CS44 https://trademarks.justia.com/883/73/rise-of-88373088.html (403; snippet): USPTO serial 88373088, design mark "RISE OF KINGDOMS" (shield, crossed swords, banner), filed 2019-04-05, goods: downloadable game software / online game services; last status date 2021-12-20 (third extension granted). Owner not shown in snippet — unverified.
- ToS 25: "All trademarks, service marks, logos, trade names ... of Lilith or our licensors ... are trademarks or registered trademarks of Lilith or our licensors."
- ToS 17.1.3 / 17.1.9 forbid using Lilith trademarks/logos (incl. "meta tags or other hidden text or metadata utilizing a Lilith ... product name") without express written consent.

- Many tools use "RoK"/"ROK" as a prefix (ROKSTATS, ROK Steward, ROK Battles, RoK Talents, MetaRoK) — also no takedown evidence found.
