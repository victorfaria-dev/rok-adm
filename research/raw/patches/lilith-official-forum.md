### 1.1.09 "A Splendid Voyage" — announced 2026-06-23, deploy 2026-06-30 06:00 UTC
URL: https://forum-global.lilithgame.com/rok/post/2259768/?lang=en_US

- New Legendary Pelagius event: "you may advance Pelagius's rarity from Epic to Legendary. The first run of this event is only available in kingdoms with servers that have opened for 5 days or more; after, this event becomes regularly available starting on the 5th day for new kingdoms."

- New event "Hidden Treasures" (lost pages from barbarians); "Simplified VFX" filter.
