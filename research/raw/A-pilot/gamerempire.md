## Claims
- G1. "it determines the maximum level of all the other buildings in your city. No other building level in the city can be higher than that of the City Hall."
- G2. Per-level requirements, e.g. "Level 5 [...] Upgrade Hospital and Wall to level 4 required. Unlocks Siege, 2nd March Queue, Shop, Workshop, and 2nd Lumber Mill. Time: 1 hour."; "Level 14 [...] Upgrade Wall, Trading post, and Alliance Center to Level 13 required."; "Level 25 [...] Cost of food and water: 82.2M each. Cost of stone: 36M. Master’s Blueprint: 1. Upgrade Trading Post and Wall to Level 24 required. Unlocks tier 5 troops. Time: 126 days, 8 hours."
- G3. Values differing from Fandom: "Level 10 [...] Cost of stone: 87,7K" (Fandom 78.7K) and "Time: 24 hours" (Fandom 22h); "Level 21 Troops: 110,000" (Fandom 100,000); "Level 22 [...] Time: 17 days, 13 hours" (Fandom 17d 3h). Matches Fandom on CH16 troops "57,000".
- G4. Ages: "Age 2: Bronze Age" before Level 4; "Age 3: Iron Age" before Level 10; "Age 4: Dark Age" before Level 16; "Age 5: Feudal Age" before Level 21.
- G5. Rewards per level: "Level 9: 50K food, wood, and stone. 1 Universal speedup (24-hour)."; "Level 10: 50K food, wood, and stone. 10K gold. 1 Civilization Change."; "Level 16: 100K stone. 20K gold. 1 Universal speedup (7-day). 100 Gems."; "Level 21: 100K Food and Wood. 200 Gems. 1 Universal speedup (7-day)."; "Level 22: 100K stone. 20K gold. 22 Building speedups (10-minute). 22 Training speedups (10-minute)."; "Level 25: 200K stone. 50K gold. 1 Master’s Blueprint. 500 Gems."
- G6. "These upgrade times show why speedups are so important in the game – without them, progress becomes very slow later on in the game."
