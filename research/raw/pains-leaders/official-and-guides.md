## Kingdom rule sites (T3 — primary evidence of how kingdoms administer)
### sites.google.com/view/jlateam/kingdom-rules/dkp (Kingdom 2779)
- "DKP is a system we use to calculate everyone's contribution in kvk."
- "DKP = T4 kill × 10 + T5 kill × 30 + death × 80" (KvK2); T1–T3 kills not counted.
- Top ~168 get best rewards; cross-season immigrants need top 80.
- Low DKP + high power (top 300) exiled after KvK; "will be zeroed if they don't leave."
- Exception: zeroed "with terrible trade" → that day's deaths count 0. Multiple accounts can combine DKP onto one.
- Site nav: Home, Immigration, Kingdom rules, Kingdom manager, Guides, AOO, Honor.
