# Interview guide — kingdom leaders (kings, council members, R5/R4, data/migration officers)

Version 1, 2026-10-13 (Sub-admin REINO, wave 2). Purpose: close the gaps that public sources cannot close. Public sources are member-heavy: Reddit via archive, tool READMEs, two kingdom rule sites. Language: English. Offer the interviewee's language if possible.

**Format:** 30–40 min, voice or text. Ask the **P1 block first**: it covers the highest uncertainty × impact on product decisions. Record kingdom number, season/stage, seed, interviewee role and timezone. Never ask for account credentials or other players' personal data (ToS 17.1.13, C02-0007). Ask for screenshots only of the interviewee's own screens or published kingdom docs.

**Screening (2 min):**
- Role and since when.
- Kingdom stage: pre-KvK, Season 1, 2, 3 or SoC.
- Number of active governors.
- Number of languages and timezones in the kingdom.

## Notes for interviewers

- Probe for **numbers** (hours, counts, money). Never suggest numbers.
- Separate what the game does (official) from what the kingdom decided.
- Ask for the **last concrete instance**, not general opinions.
- Record contradictions with our cards. Each answer can update a card's `n_fontes_pro` / `n_fontes_contra` as a T0-like primary source (tier to be agreed with the admin).
