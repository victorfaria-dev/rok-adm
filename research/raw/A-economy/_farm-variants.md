# Farm variants — extra notes for the Farm Guide (A13 scope extension)

Accessed 2026-10-13. Rule: every number has a URL + quote; "DERIVED" marks my own arithmetic/inference from cited numbers (not a source claim). Gaps listed at the end.

## (1) Gathering speed / yield per resource and tile level; main-account bottleneck by CH stage

**Per-resource speed bonuses that differ by resource (wiki, S02):**
- Academy gathering techs — first tier for food and wood comes early, for stone and gold later:
  - Sickle (food) "Increases troops food gathering speed on the map." +1% → +15% over 5 levels, Academy 5–9. https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Technology/Sickle (rev 2018-12-27)
  - Handaxe (wood) "Increases troops wood gathering speed on the map." max +15%, Academy 5–9. (rev 2018-12-27)
  - Handcart (stone) "Increases troops stone gathering speed on the map." max +15%, Academy 12–15. (rev 2024-04-04)
  - Placer Mining (gold) "Increases troops gold gathering speed on the map." max +15%, Academy 12–15. (rev 2018-12-02)
  - Scythe (food) / Whipsaw (wood) "Further increases ..." max +35%, Academy 17–20. (rev 2018-12-02)
  - Stone Saw (stone) / Shaft Mining (gold) "Further increases ..." max +35%, Academy 20–25. (rev 2020-05-14 / 2019-01-28)
  - Cutting & Polishing (gems) max +35%, Academy 20–25.
  - Map unlocks: Quarrying (stone), Metallurgy (gold, CH10), Jewelry (gems, Academy 16).
  - DERIVED: a low-CH farm can reach +15% food/wood gathering by Academy 9, but stone/gold need Academy 12–15 for their first +15% and Academy 20–25 for the second +35%. Cheap farms are therefore relatively better at food/wood; stone/gold specialization needs a more developed farm.
- Civilization gathering bonuses (wiki Civilizations, rev 2025-12-04, https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Civilizations):
  - Rome "increase food gathering speed by 10%"; France "increase wood gathering speed by 10%"; Greece "increase stone gathering speed by 10%"; Byzantium "increase stone gathering speed by 10%"; Maya "Increase Gold gathering speed by 10%"; Japan "increase resource gathering speed by 5%"; Spain "increase resource production by 20%" (buildings, not gathering).
- Commanders by resource (boostroom, 2026-09-02, WebFetch summary): Food — Wak Chanil Ajaw, Gaius Marius; Wood — Constance, Joan of Arc, Wak Chanil Ajaw; Stone — Cleopatra VII; Gold — Seondeok; flexible — Matilda, Šárka, Joan, Ishida. riseofkingdomsguides: Cleopatra "30% bonus stone gathering speed, and the remaining 20% gathering speed is for remaining resources"; Gaius "particularly good at farming food ... 20% bonus gathering speed"; Seondeok "known for gathering gold".
- Node level: allclash "the only thing that those Level 6 have compared to a Level 4 or Level 5 resource node is more resource" (no speed gain). Home map max node level 6 (near Lost Temple), Lost Kingdom up to 9 (oldmanduck).
- Gold deposits: "Gold nodes are also generally more limited than basic Food and Wood farming opportunities." (boostroom, WebFetch). Stone: "mined in quarries but more slowly and in smaller volumes than other resources" (pocketgamer) — about buildings; wiki building table confirms quarry 300/h vs farm 400/h at L1, goldmine 200/h.
- Bottleneck by stage (see A03-0021): food+wood early; stone from ~CH6 (wall, buildings); gold from CH10+ for Academy tech after Academy 10 and T3+ troops, acute at T5 ("your gold will start to melt").

## (2) Does the community specialize farms by resource?
- allclash (2019-11-23): "take France if you are about to build yourself one active farm account ... add Rome, Japan and Byzantium if you want one farm account for each resource (that’s only for players that spend a lot of time) or take Spain if you want an idle account".
  - "Rome ... If you build several farm accounts and this is one for gathering food, Rome is the best choice." "Japan ... I only recommend this if you build yourself a gold gathering account as this is the only gold gathering buff any nation gives." (OBSOLETE: Maya now has +10% gold per wiki.) "Byzantium – as for Rome and Japan, the benefits here are gathering stones."
- riseofkingdomsguides (gold guide, mod 2026-01-02): "farm gold only with your main account ... better gathering commander and technology for farming ... and to avoid transfer taxes." → argues AGAINST a gold farm. Plus theriagames: "Gold, for example, cannot be transferred between players" — if true, a gold-only farm is pointless for feeding a main (CONFLICT: heaven-guardian lists gold as transferable). Must be resolved in-game before recommending gold farms.
- Gems cannot be moved from farms → gem gathering is main-only (riseofkingdomsguides gems page, WebFetch).
- boostroom: gather the current bottleneck ("If your next upgrade requires massive amounts of Stone, gather Stone."); "keeping at least one dedicated Gold march" — march-level, not account-level, specialization.
- No source found recommending specific resource PAIRS for a farm. DERIVED candidates from mechanics: food+wood (both early techs, same CH costs, France/Rome bonuses, highest building output) — the only pair that is cheap for a low-CH farm; stone pairs need Academy 12+ for stone tech.

## (3) Transport/trade tax and limits → farm design
- Tax by Trading Post level (all sources agree): L1 35% … L10 26% … L15 21% … L20 16% … L21 15% … L22 14% … L23 12% … L24 10% … L25 8%.
- Capacity per trip CONFLICT: wiki L15 1,600,000 / L24 4,000,000 / L25 10,000,000; riseofkingdomsguides & heaven-guardian L15 800,000 / L24 2,000,000 / L25 2,500,000.
- Trading Post L10+ requires Goldmine of the same level (both sources) → a farm needs goldmines leveled to cut tax.
- "the trader will occupy a troop dispatch queue (shared with commanders)" (wiki) → every transfer trip costs a gathering march slot for its round trip; farm should sit near main (theriagames).
- Unlock: CH10 (riseofkingdomsguides CH table) vs CH12 (wiki Trading Post page) — conflict.
- heaven-guardian tiers: casual farm TP 15–16; active 21; frequent/KvK 24.
- DERIVED: at TP15 (21% tax) a farm must gather ~1.27 units per unit received; at TP24 (10%) ~1.11.
- Rss items are unplunderable and unlimited (wiki) — a farm's gathered loot arrives raw (exposed beyond storehouse protection); hence transfer frequency matters (allclash raid warning).

## (4) Daily routine and realistic daily targets
- No source gives minutes per farm per day. Qualitative only:
  - allclash: "for a mini account you don’t want to spend any more time than you have to".
  - search snippet (allclash farm page, not re-verified verbatim): farms "will help a lot with your gathering and resource income – but they are also additional work".
  - heaven-guardian (WebFetch): routine = first login, daytime refreshes, pre-logout march dispatch.
  - boostroom: send marches before stopping play to nodes big enough that the march doesn't return right away; "You do not need a complicated schedule."
  - oldmanduck: log in twice daily to keep troops gathering at the alliance resource pit.
  - Building collection: "Collect the produced resources at least once in 10 hours; otherwise, the production will stop." (pocketgamer) — consistent with wiki capacity = 10 × hourly output.
- Yield examples (single-author anecdotes): allclash "you can gather a 250,000 resource node in 45 minutes"; allclash gold "a full Level 4 Gold Deposit within one hour ... 4 armies 12 hours a day you will get about 10M" (2019; main account with boosts implied).
- GAP: no farm yield/day by CH level.

## (5) Farm setup path
- CH targets: CH11 = 3 marches; CH17 = 4; CH22 = 5 + T4 (allclash 2019; heaven-guardian 2026; riseofkingdomsguides CH table: CH5 2nd queue, CH11 3rd, CH17 4th, CH22 5th).
- heaven-guardian: "City Hall 17 is a practical minimum for a serious farm"; "City Hall 22 is the strongest general target".
- Troops: siege for load (wiki loads T1 20 vs infantry T1 7); allclash "always send Siege Units to gather".
- Commanders: only primary commander's talents apply; level 37 for Superior Tools (~25% speed) (riseofkingdomsguides); talent order (allclash).
- Research: Sickle/Handaxe early (Academy 5–9); Quarrying/Metallurgy to unlock stone/gold; Handcart/Placer Mining at Academy 12–15.
- Civ: see (2). France = Joan of Arc start + 10% wood (allclash: "Best for most people in my opinion").
- Alliance: farm in main alliance or approved farm alliance; gather inside territory (+25%).
- Trading Post up with CH; goldmines needed for TP10+.

## (6) Number of farms per spending tier
- No source gives farms-per-spending-tier. Only: allclash "one farm account for each resource (that’s only for players that spend a lot of time)" — time-based, not money-based.
