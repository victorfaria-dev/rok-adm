# F2P commanders and equipment

All commander and equipment advice here is **strategy (T3/T4)**, so confidence is media at best. Mechanics changes are T1 (C04 cards).

## Summary (≤ 10 lines)

1. **Concentrate.** Finish one complete march before starting others; partial commanders give little value [A06-0500, unanime].
2. **Sun Tzu (epic)** is the safest early F2P investment: no gold sculptures, and still useful at KvK1 [A06-0501].
   - **Sun Tzu + Aethelflaed** is the most cited F2P KvK1 lineup (T4, 2026) [A06-0501, A06-0504].
3. **Aethelflaed** is the main free legendary, from the Expedition medal store [A06-0504].
4. **Universal (gold) sculptures:** bank them for one long-term legendary. Yi Seong-Gye (YSG) is the consensus target [A06-0510, A06-0502].
5. **Gatherers:** level a gathering commander to 37 (Superior Tools) early. Never spend universal sculptures on gatherers [A06-0506, A03-0017, A06-0510].
6. **Free sculpture channels:** alliance gift chests (so join a spending alliance) [A06-0516]; VIP 10+ daily legendary sculptures [A05-0006]; Expedition medals (1,500 per legendary sculpture) [A10-0004].
7. **Equipment:** cheap green/blue pieces early, an epic set as the KvK baseline, legendary later [A07-0500]. Since 2025 the game has respec tools (Commander Swap, full-refund dismantle) [C04-0023, C04-0010].

## Verified facts (mechanics, T1/T2)

- **Starting commander depends on the civilization**, e.g. China → Sun Tzu, Spain → Pelagius [A14-0001]. A civilization change does not change the starting commander already owned [A14-0002].
- **Legendary Pelagius** upgrade event runs from **kingdom day 5** (1.1.09) [C04-0005].
- **Feb 2026 talent changes (1.1.04)** affect only damage-reduction talents and inscriptions (skill → skill/combo/smite), **Season 2+ only**. No talent-tree restructuring appears in official notes [C04-0011, A06-0514]. Early-game talent builds are not invalidated.
- **Takeda, Cyrus, Cheok and Bertrand** moved to Season 2 (1.0.94) [C04-0021].
- **Respec tools exist since 2025:**
  - Legendary Commander Swap and Gathering of Heroes (end of Season 2/3) [C04-0023].
  - Armament Conversion [C04-0023].
  - Special-talent equipment dismantle refunds 100% (1.0.96) [C04-0010].
- **Gathering:** only the primary commander's talents apply. Level 37 unlocks Superior Tools (~25% gathering speed) [A03-0017, media].
- **Daily VIP chest legendary sculptures:** 1/day at VIP10–11, 2 at VIP12–13, 3 at VIP14+ [A05-0006].

## Divergences and uncertainties (confidence baixa or divided)

- **Mid-tier legendaries** (Richard I, Charles Martel, Cao Cao): strong in KvK1 but lose value. Divided on whether F2P should spend gold sculptures on them [A06-0503].
- **Pelagius as an early pick:** "early-server monster" vs KvK1 B-tier [A06-0508]. The day-5 event (C04-0005) makes him available to every new kingdom.
- **Epic commanders' long-term value:** F/C overall in one long-horizon tier list vs S–A early [A06-0509]. The disagreement is about horizon.
- **Gatherer to level 37 first, Centurion easiest** [A06-0506, baixa].
- **Minamoto:** cheapest legendary to max, good only before SoC [A06-0507, baixa]. Acquisition route not captured.
- **Skill sweet spots** (first skill to 5; 5-5-5-1 vs expertise) [A06-0511, baixa]. No cost comparison yet.
- **Field AoE over rally/garrison commanders for F2P** [A06-0512, baixa].
- **KvK1 pair proposals vary:** Sun Tzu + Aethelflaed; Aethelflaed + Baibars; YSG + flexible; Alexander + YSG [A06-0513, dividido].
- **Aethelflaed barbarian talent build** (Quick Study → Trophy Hunter) [A06-0514, fonte_unica].
- **2026 "no priority" legendaries for F2P** (Henry, Pakal II, Tomyris, Harald, Cyrus, Gilgamesh) [A06-0515, baixa].
- **Gathering commanders by resource** [A03-0018, baixa]: Cleopatra (stone), Seondeok (gold), Gaius Marius/Ishida (food), Constance/Joan (wood/flexible).
- **Equipment details** (all baixa):
  - hoard legendary materials [A07-0501];
  - set picks by troop type [A07-0502];
  - free material sources [A07-0503];
  - legendary over epic adds little per piece, e.g. boots ~2% [A07-0504].
- **Wheel of Fortune:** 10 spins on a planned commander, after VIP 10. Gem-per-spin math obsolete where Spin Tickets apply [A11-0503, parcialmente_obsoleto].
