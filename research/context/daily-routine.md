# F2P daily routine (early kingdom)

**Minutes per task: unknown.** No guide or wiki publishes them; all were checked (A10-0002). Every "Minutes" cell below is pending the T0 stopwatch. "Value per action" is given per completion where cards quantify it. Value per minute will be computed from the T0 log.

## Summary (≤ 10 lines)

1. **Highest-value fixed daily actions (quantified):**
   - VIP daily points: 40→200, never break the streak [A05-0003].
   - Daily Objectives to 100 points: 100 gems + Golden Key + 3 epic sculptures + 170 min of speedups **(derived)** [A04-0001, A04-0010].
   - Daily AP claim: 500 AP (T1 2020) [A09-0006].
2. **Keep queues full at every login:** 2 builders, research, training. Since CH8 the queue dashboard does it from one screen [A01-0011, A01-0006].
3. **AP:** don't sit at the cap (1,500) [A09-0001]. Barbarians cost 50 AP minus 2 per consecutive hit (minimum 40) [A09-0002]. Auto-peacekeeping can run barbarian hunting [A09-0008].
4. **Gathering:** send marches inside alliance territory (+25% speed) [A03-0009]. Before logging off, use big nodes so marches don't return early [A03-0016].
5. **Weekly:** VIP Shop restock on Monday 00:00 UTC. Food-priced 100-AP items (30/week) and 5-minute speedups (50/week) cost no gems [A04-0007].

At 40 AP per barbarian after the streak discount, 3,000 AP of VIP-shop items is about 75 barbarian attacks **(derived**, A09-0006 notes).

## Recommendations (F2P, consensus)

- **Minimise logins, maximise uptime.** Refill queues and send long gathering marches before logging off [A10-0002, A03-0016, A01-0011].
- **Always claim:** VIP points (streak), daily AP, and the 100-point chest [A05-0003, A09-0006, A04-0001].
- **AP:** never cap. Barbarians early, forts as they unlock (until Castle 25). Save AP items for Lohar and Eve of the Crusade [A09-0007, majoritario].
- **Merchant / shop:** buy with resources, not gems [A04-0011].

## Divergences and uncertainties

- **Daily Objectives task list** is a 2020 wiki revision. 1.1.09 removed the boost tasks and added daily-login and alliance-donation tasks, with unknown points [A10-0001, parcialmente_obsoleto].
- **The consolidated checklist** [A10-0002, baixa] orders tasks per one commercial T3 source (LootBar).
- **A10-0007** (gathering boosts) duplicates A03-0009 and A03-0013. Its "5% to alliance storage" rests on one 2020 source [A03-0010, baixa].
- **Auto-peacekeeping:** unlock level unknown, and whether it keeps the −2 AP streak discount is unknown [A09-0008].
- **Enhanced Gathering items** (+50% gathering speed) still exist after 1.1.09; production boost items were removed [A03-0013, C04-0004].
