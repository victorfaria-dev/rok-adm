# Spending tiers: Low-tier baseline

Profiles come from `research/perfis.md`: F2P $0, Low $5–100/month, Medium $100–500, High $500–1,000, Whale $1,000–5,000, Kraken $5,000+. The guides are written as **deltas over F2P**.

**Source caveat:** the A12 cards rest on riseofkingdomsguides plus seller sites (LootBar, Packsify, heaven-guardian), all flagged CoI in `fontes.md`. No official price list was found. Prices are region- and date-dependent.

## Summary (≤ 10 lines)

1. **Growth Fund:** about **$15 once → ~81,000 gems**, paid out as City Hall milestones are reached. Full value needs CH24/25. It is the most cited first purchase [A12-0001, media].
   - **(derived)** about 5,400 gems per dollar [A12-0001 metodo].
2. **30-day gem supply ("Supply Depot"):** about **$10 per 30 days → ~19,500 gems** (650/day; must log in daily to claim) [A12-0002, media].
   - **(derived)** about 1,950 gems per dollar [A12-0002 metodo].
   - 650/day is 6.5× the F2P guaranteed 100/day [A04-0001].
3. **Low-spender discipline:** buy few high-value items, time purchases around the Recharge event, save gems for gem events (More Than Gems), don't chase every bundle [A12-0005, media].
4. **What the gems buy is the same plan as F2P, faster.** VIP is 1 gem = 1 VIP point [A05-0004]. VIP 6 → VIP 10 (daily legendary sculpture) [A05-0008, A05-0006]. Spend during More Than Gems [A11-0502].

## Verified facts relevant to pricing value

- **VIP:** points = gems 1:1 [A05-0004]. Thresholds (cumulative reading): VIP6 11,500; VIP10 150,000; VIP12 350,000; VIP14 750,000 [A05-0001]. Daily legendary sculptures: 1/day at VIP10, 2 at VIP12, 3 at VIP14 [A05-0006].
- **Growth Fund timing:** gems accrue with CH progress, so buying early loses nothing [A12-0001]. CH rewards add 100/200/500 gems at CH16/21/25 [A01-0002].
- **F2P baseline to compare against:** 100 guaranteed gems/day [A04-0001].

## Divergences and uncertainties (baixa)

- **Cheap $5 offers cited as good value** [A12-0003, baixa]:
  - 7-day material supply;
  - Vanquisher bundle;
  - a "Gem Supply 50% off" at ~21,700 gems (LootBar), which may conflict with A12-0002's ~19,500 per $10 (possibly a first-purchase variant).
- **VIP points per dollar in one recorded Call of the Ancients table:** $5→525, $10→1,000, $20→2,000, $50→4,000, $100→6,000. VIP/$ falls at higher tiers **(derived)**: about 105 per dollar at $5 vs 60 at $100 [A12-0004, baixa, A12-0004 metodo]. Source is a top-up seller; quote not literal.
- **First-purchase gem multiplier** returned on 2026-01-01 (C04-0032). Its value is not researched.
- **Region / currency:** all prices are USD as cited by third parties. Brazil (BRL) prices are unknown.
