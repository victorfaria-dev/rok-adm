# Game state (Oct 2026) and patch timeline

Raw evidence: `research/raw/patches/` (`lilith-official-forum.md`, `ldshop-gg.md`, `community-misc.md`, `_sources.md`). Fact cards: `research/facts/C04/`.

## Resumo (≤ 10 linhas)

- **Version on 2026-10-13: probably 1.1.12 "Dreams of Phantasma" (launched 2026-09-22), but that is only reported by a third-party source.** The newest version with official English patch notes is **1.1.09 (deployed 2026-06-30)**. Lilith has posted no English patch notes on its forum since 2026-06-23 (C04-0001).
- Lilith ships a major version roughly every month. A new Season of Conquest "key story" arrives each October (King of All Britain in 2024, Song of Troy in 2025). The 2026 key story is not confirmed. Mighty Assembly (Jul 2026, T3) is a candidate.
- **2024–2026 direction:** new formations and armament systems, Season 2–SoC catch-up tools, and less daily busywork.
  - Formation and armament systems: transmutation, conversion, honing, formations focused on smite, combo or skill damage (C04-0012, C04-0023, C04-0029).
  - Season 2–SoC catch-up tools: Commander Swap, Gathering of Heroes, commanders moved to Season 2 (C04-0021, C04-0023).
  - Less daily busywork: queue panel, auto-peacekeeping, removal of resource-boost items (C04-0004, C04-0032).
  - KvK structure and admin tools: Kingdom Merger, Acclaim, Army Groups, Rest Mode vote, Conduct Score anti-script system (C04-0006, C04-0013, C04-0015, C04-0031).
- **Highest-impact changes for a new-kingdom F2P guide:**
  - jumping is removed (C04-0026)
  - resource boosts are gone and VIP production bonuses are higher (C04-0004)
  - Legendary Pelagius is available from day 5 (C04-0005)
  - Season 3 is moving to King of All Britain (C04-0007)
  - immigration rules are tighter (C04-0008)
  - Wheel of Fortune needs Spin Tickets, which may reach Season 1 (C04-0022)
  - the talent rework of Feb 2026 (C04-0011).

## Commonly repeated advice that is now outdated

Each item names the change and card that makes it outdated. Status: (T1) = confirmed by official notes; (T3) = third-party only.

1. **"Make a jumper account / use Beginner's Immigration to start in a better new kingdom."** Distribution stopped on 2025-03-26 (C04-0026, media).
2. **"Use 24h resource boosts every day; buy them in the VIP/Courier shop; use Deceptive Troops."** These items were removed on 2026-06-30. VIP now gives larger production bonuses (C04-0004, T1). Daily checklists and quest lists that include "use a resource boost" are wrong.
3. **"VIP caps at 18."** VIP19 and SVIP have existed since Oct 2024 (C04-0024, T1). VIP production percentages changed again in 2026 (C04-0004).
4. **"Season 3 = Light and Darkness."** King of All Britain has been piloting as Season 3 since May 2026 and appears to be standard by Sep 2026 (C04-0007, media).
5. **"SoC = Heroic Anthem / Strife of the Eight / March of the Ages."** There are more than ten rotating stories now (C04-0017). Heroic Anthem is only the first-run story.
6. **"Spin the Wheel of Fortune with gems directly (X gems per spin)."** Spin Tickets are required: Season 3/SoC since Mar 2025, Season 2 since May 2025, and Season 1 since Sep 2026 per T3 (C04-0022). Older gem-per-spin calculations are wrong.
7. **Talent builds made before Feb 2026 that use "skill damage reduction" talents** (Loose Formation, Heraldic Shield, Saving Cross, etc.) undervalue them. These talents now also reduce combo and smite damage from Season 2 on (C04-0011, T1).
8. **"Takeda / Cyrus / Cheok / Bertrand are SoC-only."** They have been Season 2 commanders since May 2025, and Cheok's and Bertrand's kits changed (C04-0021, T1).
9. **"Sculptures and armaments are locked in forever, so never invest in the wrong one."** Since 2025 the game has Legendary Commander Swap (C04-0023), Armament Conversion (C04-0023), a full-refund equipment dismantle (C04-0010) and armament honing (C04-0012). Respec is possible, though limited and costly.
10. **"There's no way to turn excess resources into gems."** The Oasis Bazaar sells resources for Gems (event since Oct 2025; permanent in SoC since Mar 2026, pioneer) (C04-0009, T1).
11. **"Don't claim daily AP when near the cap."** Since Dec 2025 the claim can exceed the cap (C04-0032, T1).
12. **"Zero / attack farms inside other alliances' territory at home during SoC."** This has been blocked since Jan 2026 (C04-0014, T1).
13. **"Bots or scripts for barbarians and gathering are low-risk."** The in-game Conduct Score has penalised third-party tools since May 2026, and the game now has built-in auto-peacekeeping (C04-0006, T1).
14. **Immigration numbers from guides older than Mar 2026** (240/120/180-day age limits, power caps of 100M or more) are out of date (C04-0008, C04-0025, T1).
15. **Kingdom power / Imperium math** (old flat 150M cap per governor) was replaced in Jun 2025 (C04-0020, T1).
16. **SoC event-prep guides that plan around Now or Never, Barbarian Buster, Lord of War, Territory Grab, Strategic Reserves or Enemy Elimination.** These events were removed or replaced in 2024–2025 (C04-0030, T1).
17. **Civilization tables** that lack Maya, or that say civilization bonuses can't be swapped separately, are out of date. Buff switching unlocks at CH16 since Nov 2025 (C04-0016, T1).
18. **Crystal tech research orders written before mid-2025** use the old tree (C04-0018, T1).
19. **"Pelagius is an Epic commander only."** A Legendary upgrade event has existed since Jun 2026 and starts on day 5 of new kingdoms (C04-0005, T1).

## Divergências e incertezas

- **Current version:** 1.1.12 is based on T3 only. Official confirmation is missing for 1.1.10–1.1.12 (C04-0001).
- **Season 3 story:** the official pioneer is May 2026; the claim that it is now standard comes from T3 (C04-0007).
- **Season 1 story name:** "Endless War" is inferred from official story lists, not stated as "Season 1".
- **Plans versus releases:** dev Q&A plans can differ from what ships. Example: Alliance Fortress max durability was announced as 100,000 but released at 124,000 (1.0.95). Treat Q&A posts as intent only.
- **Pioneer features:** many 2025–2026 changes start as a "Pioneer experience in select kingdoms" (Oasis Bazaar permanent access, same-season immigration lock, Rest Mode vote, Camp Treaty, Radiant Rivalry). They may not be live in a given kingdom.
- **Version names:** the official English forum shows no 1.0.97 or 1.1.05 notes.
