# Early kingdom timeline and events (first ~90–100 days)

Main sections: confianca ≥ media. Official changes come from C04 (T1). The C04 cards are maintained by the patch front; the four used here (Pelagius day 5, 3 starting provinces, end of Beginner's Immigration, Chronicles durations) were re-read in the official forum corpus on 2026-10-13.

## Summary (≤ 10 lines)

1. A new kingdom spends roughly its first **85–100 days in the Preparation Season.** Pre-KvK (Eve of the Crusade) starts around day 85–95; KvK1 around day ~100 [B01-0500; T4 agrees: "the first 90 days or so"].
2. **Kingdom Chronicles (Monument)** paces the kingdom. Chapters show a default duration and can end early when their objective is met (T1) [B01-0505]. Stage-by-stage dates are still T3/undated.
3. **Official changes affecting a 2026 new kingdom:**
   - 3 starting provinces instead of 6 (1.0.78) [C04-0033];
   - no Beginner's Immigration (jumping) since 2025-03-26 [C04-0026];
   - **Legendary Pelagius event from day 5** (1.1.09) [C04-0005];
   - Radiant Rivalry, a 1v1 kingdom event, as a pioneer in some new kingdoms (T3) [C04-0003].
4. **Events are not on a fixed schedule.** Plan from the in-game calendar, which looks a few days ahead [A11-0512].
5. **F2P priorities:** take MGE milestones, not ranking [A11-0500]; hoard speedups, AP and resource items for scored events and KvK [A11-0501]; Lohar/Arms Training is high value, so save AP potions for it [A11-0505]; More Than Gems is the window for gems → VIP (~monthly) [A11-0502].
6. **Before KvK1:** AP and speedup stockpiles for **Eve of the Crusade** marauders [A09-0007]. In Season 1 the Lost Kingdom now opens together with pre-KvK (1.1.12, T3) [B01-0503, C04-0002].

## Verified facts

### Lifecycle and timing

- **Stage order:** Preparation Season → Season 1 (KvK1) → Season 2 → Season 3 → Season of Conquest. Season 3 is moving to King of All Britain (pioneer since May 2026) [C04-0007]. Full table and story list: `context/game-state-and-patch-timeline.md`.
- **KvK1 timing:** pre-KvK at about day 85–95, KvK1 at about day 100 [B01-0500, media]. Corroborated by T4 (2026): "The first 90 days or so you are building a kingdom" (same card).
- **Kingdom Chronicles:** default duration shown per chapter; chapters end early when their objective is achieved (1.0.88, T1) [B01-0505].
- **Barbarians:** only Lv1–6 exist at kingdom opening; higher levels unlock kingdom-wide over the following days. Lv ≤12 can be spawned next to the city [A09-0004].
- **Peerless Scholar** (Lyceum, CH10): mock exam Mon–Fri; prelim and final every two weeks; a gem pool is split among those who pass [A10-0005, alta].
- **Events confirmed live in 2026** [A11-0513, media]:
  - by T1 patch notes: Sunset Canyon (Jan 2026), Champions of Olympia (Mar 2026), Arms Training with Lohar (Apr 2026);
  - by 2026 T3: Mightiest Governor, Wheel of Fortune, More Than Gems, Golden Kingdom, Shadow Legion, Ceroli Crisis, Alliance Mobilization, Karuak Ceremony, Ark of Osiris and more.

## Divergences and uncertainties

- **Monument stage table** (21 stages from "The Bud Awakens" to "The Last Golden Apple", summing to about 85 days at maximum duration) is from an undated T3 table [B01-0505, baixa]. Durations are maxima (T1).
- **KvK durations:**
  - 60 days for KvK1 per a 2024 T3 source;
  - "all KvKs last 50 days" per a 2026 T4 comment.

  Contested [B01-0502]. Heroic Anthem was set to 50 days in 1.0.76 (C04).
- **Lost Kingdom entry needs CH17** (single T3). Eve timing changed: in SoC, Eve starts inside the Lost Kingdom (T1 1.0.95); in Season 1, pre-KvK overlaps the Lost Kingdom opening (1.1.12, T3 plus a T4 report) [B01-0503, parcialmente_obsoleto].
- **Season list in B01-0501** was partially wrong ("Tides of War" exists; Season 3 is moving to King of All Britain) [B01-0501, parcialmente_obsoleto]. Use the C04 timeline instead.
- **KvK matchmaking by kingdom age** [B01-0504, baixa].
- **New kingdoms are hardest for F2P early;** the first 60–90 days set the hierarchy [B01-0506, baixa].
- **Player reports on the F2P state at KvK1:** about CH21, T4 by about day 30, 10–20M power [B01-0700, baixa].
- **Wheel of Fortune:** Spin Tickets in Season 1 since 1.1.12 (T3) [C04-0022]. Home-kingdom pricing unknown [A11-0503].
- **Event value opinions:**
  - Ark of Osiris for F2P: divided [A11-0508].
  - Champions of Olympia "most F2P-friendly": single source [A11-0509].
  - Priority list (Lohar, Karuak, Golden Kingdom, Ceroli, Alliance Mobilization, Shadow Legion) [A11-0506, baixa].
