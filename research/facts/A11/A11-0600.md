Official event descriptions: Gem Rush 'Make in-game purchases to earn extra gifts' runs only in regions where all kingdoms are over 21 days old and before Season 2; Road to Greatness ('recharge to earn rewards') runs during or after Season 2; Recharge Rewards ('Recharge daily and earn incredible rewards') appeared in the 1.0.94 Museum Day series. Reward tables and thresholds are not in the notes.
