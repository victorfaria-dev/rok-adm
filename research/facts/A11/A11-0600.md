# Official recharge-type events: Gem Rush (21+ day kingdoms, before Season 2), Road to Greatness / Epic Glory (Season 2+ counterparts), Recharge Rewards (recharge daily)

Official event descriptions: Gem Rush ('Make in-game purchases to earn extra gifts') runs only in regions where all kingdoms are over 21 days old and before Season 2 (1.0.82 and 1.0.86 notes); for regions in Season 2 or later the 1.0.82 note lists Road to Greatness ('recharge to earn rewards') and the 1.0.86 note lists Epic Glory ('Make purchases to get gifts!'); in 1.0.87 Road to Greatness ('Purchase Gems to summon your favorite commander') carried only the 21-day note. Recharge Rewards ('Recharge daily and earn incredible rewards') appeared in the 1.0.94 Museum Day series. Reward tables and thresholds are not in the notes.
