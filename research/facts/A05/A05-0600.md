# VIP Special Privileges: one-time paid pack per VIP level, $0.99 (VIP 0-3) to $99.99; VIP 0-9 packs carry Minamoto sculptures (10 to 270)

Each VIP level has a 'Special Privileges' pack that can be bought once: $0.99 at VIP 0-3, $4.99 at VIP 4-5, $9.99 at VIP 6, $19.99 at VIP 7, $49.99 at VIP 8, $99.99 at VIP 9, $19.99 at VIP 10-12, $49.99 at VIP 13 and $99.99 at VIP 14-18. VIP 0-9 packs contain Minamoto no Yoshitsune sculptures (10, 10, 10, 10, 20, 40, 70, 100, 150, 270), which a 2021 analysis says are only available through these packs.
