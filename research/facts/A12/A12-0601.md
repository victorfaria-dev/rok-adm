# 30-day Gem Supply: ~2,200 gems on purchase + 650/day for 30 days (~21,700 total); 19,500 counts only the daily part

The 30-day Gem Supply (Supply Depot) pays 650 gems per day for 30 days (19,500) and, per player and seller sources, also an upfront amount on purchase/renewal (2,200 per a 2026 player; 2,000 per a seller). 2,200 + 19,500 = 21,700, which matches the '~21,700 gems' figure that two sources give for the pack.
