# Writer of History pop-up: $5 when a new legendary commander is acquired; ~1,050 gems, 525 VIP and 10 sculptures of that commander

The Writer of History pop-up appears each time a new legendary commander is acquired and costs $5; LootBar lists 1,050 gems, 600,000 EXP, 10 legendary stars, 525 VIP and 10 legendary sculptures, but its own multi-pack lines imply 1,100 gems and 500 VIP per pack ('x8 ... $40 ~8.800 Gems, 4000 VIP'; 'x11 ... $55 ~12.100 Gems, 5.500 VIP'). Players recommend buying it every time ('Always buy 5$ legendary commander unlock').
