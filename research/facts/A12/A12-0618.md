# Value-per-dollar method for the Low planner: raw gems (and VIP) per USD is defensible; item valuation only as a labelled range

Only raw gems (and VIP points) per USD can be computed defensibly from the cards: Growth Fund 81,000 / 15 = 5,400 gems/$ (but ~40,000 of it only at CH25); 30-day supply 19,500 / 9.99 = 1,952 gems/$ (2,172 with the 2,200 upfront); one-time half-price supply 21,700 / 5 = 4,340 gems/$; super-value bundles 210 gems/$ and 105 VIP/$ at $5 down to 250 gems/$ and 60 VIP/$ at $100 (50 VIP/$ with the 5,000-VIP variant); Lucerne $5 tier 660 gems/$ and 1,100 VIP/$. Valuing speedups, sculptures or materials in gems changes rankings by up to ~5x depending on the basis (1-h speedup = 25 gems community vs 120 gems Shop), and the in-game '% value' labels use undiscounted prices, so item-inclusive 'value per dollar' should be shown only as a labelled range.
