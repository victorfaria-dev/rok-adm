# Daily Special Offer (official): three daily offers with chosen-commander sculptures, from kingdom day 43; an extra advanced bundle after buying all three

Official notes: there are three Daily Special Offers (2021 Mega Pack discount event); since 1.0.42 the player chooses the commander whose sculptures they contain, with more options as the kingdom ages; Daily Special Offers open on day 43 of the Preparation Season (1.0.71); since 1.0.98 buying all Daily Special Offer bundles in a day unlocks a limited-time advanced bundle with guaranteed sculptures. In April 2025 the developers said the regular price would be lowered to the discounted price.
