# Daily Special Offer (official): three daily offers with chosen-commander sculptures, from kingdom day 43; an extra advanced bundle after buying all three

Official notes: there are three Daily Special Offers (2021 Mega Pack discount event); since 1.0.42 the player chooses the commander whose sculptures they contain, with more options as the kingdom ages; a 1.0.71 note about Pyrrhus being added to them says Daily Special Offers and Card King 'will be available starting on day 43' of the Preparation Season (it may refer only to Pyrrhus' availability there); since 1.0.98 buying all Daily Special Offer bundles in a day unlocks a limited-time advanced bundle with guaranteed sculptures. In April 2025 the developers said they were 'planning to adjust the price of Daily Special Offers to match the discounted price' (date to be announced in-game; the notes found do not confirm it happened). Since 1.1.01 (Nov 2025) advanced offers appear each time a commander Daily Special Offer is added.
