# 30-day Gem Supply must be claimed daily; unclaimed days are forfeited (single seller source)

One seller guide states that the 30-day supply's gems are claimed by daily login and that unclaimed days are forfeited ('Missed days are gone'). Guides agree the gems are paid daily over the month; no source found that says missed days are mailed or accumulated.
