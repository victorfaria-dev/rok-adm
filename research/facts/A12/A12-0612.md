# 7-day (weekly) Material Supply: ~$5 / $4.99; gems per pack conflict (1,000 vs ~1,050 vs ~700) plus materials

The 7-day Material Supply costs about $5 ($4.99 in a player post) and is described as 1,000 gems plus two legendary materials (riseofkingdomsguides) or, per LootBar, ~1,050 gems + 140 green materials per pack (x4 for $20: ~4,200 gems, 560 green materials) but ~700 per pack in its x12 line ($60: ~8,400 gems). Players buy it 4-5 times a month.
