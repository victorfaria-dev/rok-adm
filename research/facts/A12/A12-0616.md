# Shop list prices in gems (Fandom, rev. 2023): 60-min speedup 120, 24-h 1,500, 7-day 10,000; VIP point items cost 1 gem per point

The Shop sells items for gems at fixed list prices: Universal Speedups 1 min = 3, 15 min = 40, 60 min = 120, 3 h = 300, 8 h = 600, 15 h = 1,000, 24 h = 1,500, 3 days = 4,400, 7 days = 10,000, 30 days = 40,000 gems; VIP point items 10/100/500/1,000/5,000 cost the same number of gems; 50% troop expansion items ('Advanced Army Expansion') 5,000. The VIP Shop sells some items cheaper with weekly limits.
