# Super-value bundle ladder (Call of the Ancients, King’s Coronation, etc.): $5/$10/$20/$50/$100 tiers give 1,050/2,200/4,600/12,000/25,000 gems and 525/1,000/2,000/4,000 VIP, then 6,000 (rkg 2022) or 5,000 (LootBar 2026) VIP at $100

riseofkingdomsguides (page published 2022-01-14) tabulates ten super-value bundle families (Call of the Ancients, King's Coronation, City of Hope, Fountain of Wisdom, War Machine, Resource Reserve, Fate Changer, Mighty Conqueror/End of the Earth and two others) with the same price ladder $5, $10, $20, $50, $100, $100, $100 ($385 total) and the same gem/VIP rows: 1,050 / 2,200 / 4,600 / 12,000 / 25,000 gems and 525 / 1,000 / 2,000 / 4,000 / 6,000 VIP points (5,000 at $100 in one family, Mighty Conqueror/End of the Earth, and in the 5-tier $185 Hammer and Anvil). Call of the Ancients resets every Sunday. LootBar (2026-03) gives the same gem rungs and 525..4,000 VIP, but prices King's Coronation 'Full' at $185 with ~44,850 gems and 12,525 VIP, i.e. five tiers with 5,000 VIP at $100 (derived), not rkg's seven tiers with 6,000.
