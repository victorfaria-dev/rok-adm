# Armament supply is season-gated: armament chests from Season 2, transmutation/supply extras from Season 3

Official notes put most armament supply in Season 2+. Since 1.0.71 (Jul 2023), kingdoms in Season 2 through SoC get Formation Choice Chests and/or Random Armament Chests from Lucerne Scrolls, Silk Road, civilization events and Lost Kingdom events. Season 2–SoC Crusader Achievements add Formation Choice Chests (1.0.72). Transmutation Stones come from Crusader Achievements in Season 3/SoC. The In Search of Wonders armament event was announced as Season 3/SoC only through 1.0.88 (Oct 2024), and a 7-Day Armament Supply bundle as Season 3/SoC only (1.0.80).
