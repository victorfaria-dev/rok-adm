# Iconic Crystals: limited supply from achievements (12 Champion, up to 3 SoC Crusader) — Iconic needs Season 1+

Awakening a Legendary piece to Iconic I costs Iconic Crystals and Gold. Higher Iconic tiers (up to V) cost blueprints, materials and Gold, and Iconic needs the kingdom in Season 1 or later (1.0.77). Official crystal sources are achievement-based. 12 equipment-related 'Champion' achievements give 1 Iconic Crystal each (1.0.60). SoC Crusader achievements give up to 3 (1.0.59). Choosing which pieces get crystals is therefore a scarce, long-term decision.
