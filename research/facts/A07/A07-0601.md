# Iconic Crystals: official sources are achievements, Artisan's Forge, Crystal Quest and the Lost Canyon Shop; Iconic needs Season 1+

Awakening a Legendary piece to Iconic I costs Iconic Crystals and Gold. Higher Iconic tiers (up to V) cost blueprints, materials and Gold, and Iconic needs the kingdom in Season 1 or later (1.0.77). Official crystal sources: the Artisan's Forge event, purchase in the Lost Canyon Shop and Crystal Quest rewards (1.0.56, Mar 2022); 12 equipment-related 'Champion' achievements at 1 crystal each (1.0.60); and SoC Crusader achievements (1 crystal in 1.0.57, up to 3 more in 1.0.59). Total crystals an F2P can collect is not stated.
