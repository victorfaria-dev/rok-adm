# SoC adds legendary equipment channels: Season Shop legendary material chests, Combat Shop accessories, SoC blueprints

SoC kingdoms get extra equipment channels that Seasons 1–3 lack. Since 1.0.55 (Mar 2022), the Season Shop sells discounted Legendary sculptures and Legendary Equipment Material Choice Chests. Since 1.0.94 (May 2025), the new Legendary smite/combo accessories (Saddle of a Thousand Blessings, Hero's Hilt) sit in the Blacksmith and in the SoC Combat Shop and Holy Knight's Treasure. SoC-specific blueprint-fragment rewards also exist, e.g. engineering blueprints in SoC event chests (1.0.81). Before SoC, epic engineering blueprints enter Tavern Equipment Chests after the Season 2 Eve of the Crusade (1.0.91).
