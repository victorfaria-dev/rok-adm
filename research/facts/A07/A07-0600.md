# 13 Season-of-Conquest-only equipment pieces (2021 rule): KvK-shop legendaries don't work before SoC

Since Jul 2021 these 13 equipment items are restricted to Season of Conquest: Sacred Dominion, Hammer of the Sun and Moon, The Hydra's Blast, Pride of the Khan, Helm of the Conqueror, Ancestral Mask of Night, Scolas' Lucky Coin, Ring of Doom, Greatest Glory, Horn of Fury, Concealed Dagger, Mora's Web and Vengeance. Kingdoms in Seasons 1–3 should not plan builds around them.
