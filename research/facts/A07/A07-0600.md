# 13 Season-of-Conquest-only equipment pieces (2021 rule): KvK-shop legendaries don't work before SoC

In Jul 2021 Lilith announced that 13 equipment items are restricted to Season of Conquest: Sacred Dominion, Hammer of the Sun and Moon, The Hydra's Blast, Pride of the Khan, Helm of the Conqueror, Ancestral Mask of Night, Scolas' Lucky Coin, Ring of Doom, Greatest Glory, Horn of Fury, Concealed Dagger, Mora's Web and Vengeance. The announcement does not say whether the restriction is on use or on acquisition, and no later official text re-confirms it. 2026 players describe these 'KvK' pieces as bought with Conquest Coins once the kingdom reaches SoC.
