# Iconic attributes scale with the kingdom's season group (S1–2 / S3 / SoC)

Official 1.0.56 notes (Mar 2022) say Iconic equipment buffs troops' base attributes according to the kingdom's current Lost Kingdom season. Per a third-party guide that reproduces in-game text, these bonuses are grouped as Seasons 1–2, Season 3 and SoC and change on entering a new season or on migrating; Iconic stat lines per crystal grow from 1 (S1–2) to 2 (S3) to 3 (SoC); refined pieces with a special talent get +30% Iconic attributes.
