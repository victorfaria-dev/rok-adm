# Iconic attributes scale with the kingdom's season group (S1–2 / S3 / SoC)

Per a third-party guide that reproduces in-game text, Iconic base-attribute bonuses change automatically with the kingdom's Lost Kingdom season, grouped as Seasons 1–2, Season 3 and SoC. They change on entering a new season or on migrating. Per the same guide, Iconic stat lines per crystal grow from 1 (S1–2) to 2 (S3) to 3 (SoC). Refined pieces with a special talent get +30% Iconic attributes.
