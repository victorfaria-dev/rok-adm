# Crystal technology rework (2025)

A Crystal tech rework was pioneered from 1.0.94 (May 2025) and extended in 1.0.96/1.0.98/1.0.99/1.1.00/1.1.02 to most SoC stories: earlier Unit Capacity, invest only in preferred unit type, Tough Skin/Mantlets/Pike Wall/Shatterproof replaced by Infantry/Cavalry/Archer/Siege Expert, Rapid Retreat auto mode (instant return 200→80 Gems).
