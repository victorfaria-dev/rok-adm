# Legendary equipment respec: special talent reset and full-refund dismantle

Since 1.0.96 (Jul 2025) dismantling Legendary equipment with a special talent refunds 100% of forging cost and 50% of awakening cost. 1.1.06 added limited-time events (from Apr 2026) to reset a Legendary item's special talent for Gems and to dismantle one Legendary item for Gems with all materials returned.
