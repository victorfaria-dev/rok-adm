# 1.0.89: Hall of Heroes refunds part of KvK healing resources; Realm of Mystique

Since 1.0.89 (Dec 2024) the Hall of Heroes also returns part of the resources spent healing in the Lost Kingdom at its end (Lost Kingdoms starting after the update). New co-op PvE mode Realm of Mystique added.
