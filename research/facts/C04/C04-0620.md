# 1.0.77: King of the Nile healing seasonal strategies capped per season (Frugality 700M resources, Medical Skills 6,000 min, Safe Return 500k units)

Since 1.0.77 (Dec 2023) the King of the Nile seasonal strategies keep their effects but are capped per season: Frugality (−10% healing resources) saves at most 700,000,000 resources, Medical Skills (+15% healing speed) at most 6,000 minutes, and Safe Return (−8% severely wounded become slightly wounded) at most 500,000 units; Safe Return does not affect dead units.
