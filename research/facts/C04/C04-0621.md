# 1.0.45: Hall of Heroes troops returned at SoC end even if offline; siege units get a separate healing zone outside hospital capacity

Update 1.0.45 (Apr 2021) made Hall of Heroes troops return at the end of the Season of Conquest regardless of whether the governor is online, and added a siege unit zone in addition to the hospital so that a number of siege units can be healed without impacting hospital capacity (stated purpose: basic gathering needs).
