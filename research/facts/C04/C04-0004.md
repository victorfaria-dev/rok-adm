# 1.1.09: resource boost items and Deceptive Troops removed; VIP production buffs raised

Since 1.1.09 (2026-06-30) all 8h/24h Food/Wood/Stone/Gold resource boost items and Deceptive Troops (24h) were removed from the game and inventories (converted to resources / Anti-Scouting Spyglass). VIP resource production buffs were raised: VIP0–5 = +5/+10/+15/+20/+28/+37%; VIP>5 +25% relative to old values. Daily quests requiring boost items were replaced by login and alliance donation quests.
