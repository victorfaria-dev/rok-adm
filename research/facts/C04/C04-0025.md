# 1.0.83: cross-season immigration Gem deposit and power limits

Since 1.0.83 (Jun 2024) cross-season immigration requires a refundable 20,000 Gem deposit when eligibility is granted (lost if not used), governors under 10M power are ineligible, and the power limit was lowered from 100M to 85M.
