# 1.0.77: Iconic equipment awakening (5 tiers)

Since 1.0.77 (Dec 2023) all Legendary equipment except Trial of the Lost Kingdom and gathering equipment can be awakened into Iconic equipment with five tiers; requires kingdom in Season 1 or later.
