# 1.1.03: Armament honing (Legendary armaments)

Since 1.1.03 (2026-01-20) Legendary armaments can be honed: +0.2% per hone up to +3.5% for unit Attack/Defense/Health/March Speed, or +0.1% per hone up to +2.0% for all damage, using Honing Stones only from the Autarch-limited section of the Lost Kingdom Inscription Shop.
