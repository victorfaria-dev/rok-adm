# 1.0.95: kingdom power formula and Imperium threshold

Since 1.0.95 (Jun 2025) a governor's contribution to kingdom power counts 100% up to 150M, 80% for 150–300M, 50% for 300–500M, and nothing above 500M (top 300 governors). SoC Imperium Kingdom power threshold set to 23.5B.
