Wheel of Fortune spins require Spin Tickets bought with Gems or bundles: Season 3 and SoC since 1.0.92 (Mar 2025, SoC pool swapped construction for healing speedups), Season 2 since 1.0.94 (May 2025), and reportedly Season 1 since 1.1.12 (Sep 2026, T3). 1.1.03 added multi-spin.
