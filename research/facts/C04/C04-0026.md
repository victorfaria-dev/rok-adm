# Beginner's Immigration (jumping) removed for new accounts from 2025-03-26

Lilith suspended distribution of Beginner's Immigration items to new characters from 2025-03-26, ending the 'jumper account' practice of moving a fresh character to another new kingdom; the item is listed as unobtainable.
