# Acclaim: KvK contribution currency (launched 1.1.01, tuned 1.1.04)

From 1.1.01 (Nov 2025) governors in new SoC Lost Kingdoms earn Acclaim from severe wounds/kills inflicted and suffered, scaled by troop level, and some Crusader Achievements require Acclaim. 1.1.04 set Lvl1 units to 1 Acclaim per 10,000 severely wounded and 2 per 10,000 killed (anti T1-trading). 1.1.04 added an Acclaim Shop (pioneer) with Recovery Potions.
