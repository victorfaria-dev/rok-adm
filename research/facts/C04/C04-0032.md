# 1.1.02: daily AP claim can exceed cap; Army Groups for all SoC alliances

Since 1.1.02 (2025-12-18) Daily Action Points can be claimed whenever current AP is below cap and the total may exceed the cap; all alliances in Season of Conquest get Army Groups (one officer controls troops assigned by members); SoC 100-activity chest gives a Sovereign Key instead of Golden Key.
