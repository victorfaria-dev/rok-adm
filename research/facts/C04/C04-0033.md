# Kingdom lifecycle: Preparation Season → Season 1 → Season 2 → Season 3 → Season of Conquest

Official patch notes refer to a new kingdom's stages as Preparation Season, Season 1, Season 2 ('Distant Journey'), Season 3 ('Light and Darkness', with King of All Britain piloting in 2026) and then Season of Conquest (rotating stories, first-run story Heroic Anthem). Story registration for SoC needs City Hall 25; after a SoC season ends, registration for the next can begin immediately.
