# 1.1.03: Kingdom Merger, Lost Kingdom tiers and SoC coalition changes

Since 1.1.03: Kingdom Merger teams need min 2 kingdoms (was 3) and 150 active governors (was 200); team power = top 300 governors, capped at top-24-kingdom power. Lost Kingdom tiers became A, A+, S, S+. SoC coalitions start with 4 alliances (was 2). Camps can reject matchmaking with >80% votes; kingdoms at skip limit are forced into a by-election story.
