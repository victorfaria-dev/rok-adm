# 1.0.90: Armament Conversion, Legendary Commander Swap, Gathering of Heroes

1.0.90 (Jan 2025) added Armament Conversion (Conversion Stones convert a Legendary armament to the same slot of another formation), the Legendary Commander Swap event (swap levels, skill levels and stars of two Legendary commanders with Swap Tickets) and Gathering of Heroes (points to recruit unowned Legendaries). Dev Q&A: swap event targeted at new kingdoms around end of Season 2, once per character.
