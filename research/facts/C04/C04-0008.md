# 1.1.06: immigration age limits tightened; same-season immigration restricted for new kingdoms

Since 1.1.06 (2026-03-24): character-age limits for immigration were cut — SoC first story same-season 240→150 days older max; cross-season to Season 2 120→80 days; to Season 3 180→120 days. New kingdoms are restricted from same-season immigration until end of Season 1 (pioneer on select continents).
