# Oasis Bazaar: sell resources for Gems (event 1.1.00, permanent in SoC from 1.1.06)

Oasis Bazaar lets governors buy resources with Clamyks or sell resources for Gems, with weekly limits and season-dependent prices. Introduced as an event in 1.1.00 (Oct 2025); from 1.1.06 (Mar 2026) SoC kingdoms access it regularly via Trading Post Lv25 (pioneer in select kingdoms).
