# 1.0.94: four commanders moved to Season 2 and reworked

Since 1.0.94 (May 2025) Takeda Shingen, Cyrus the Great, Cheok Jun-gyeong and Bertrand du Guesclin are Season 2 commanders (Wheel of Fortune, Daily Special Offer, Tavern odds); Cheok talents became Infantry/Versatility/Smite and Bertrand Cavalry/Versatility/Combo with reworked skills; they can no longer be swapped in the Legendary Commander Swap.
