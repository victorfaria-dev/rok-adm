# 1.1.04: talent and inscription damage-reduction rework (skill → skill/combo/smite)

Since 1.1.04 (2026-02-27), in Season 2 and beyond, every 'reduction to skill damage taken' effect on talents and armament inscriptions now also reduces combo attack and smite damage; some combo/smite talents were buffed (e.g. Colossus 6% combo, Enhanced Smite 6% smite).
