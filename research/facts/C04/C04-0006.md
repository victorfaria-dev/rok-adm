# 1.1.07: Conduct Score anti-script/cheat system

Since 1.1.07 (2026-05-07) players using third-party tools such as scripts or cheats lose Conduct Score; below thresholds penalties apply. First rolled out in Season of Conquest kingdoms. 1.1.10 reportedly added a monthly Good Conduct Reward.
