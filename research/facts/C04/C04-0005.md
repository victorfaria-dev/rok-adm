# 1.1.09: Legendary Pelagius event for new kingdoms from day 5

Since 1.1.09 a 'Legendary Pelagius' event lets governors upgrade Pelagius from Epic to Legendary; it becomes regularly available from day 5 of new kingdoms. 1.1.09 also added the 'Duel of Heroes' event to Heroic Anthem.
