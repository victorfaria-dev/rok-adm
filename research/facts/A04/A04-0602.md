# Universal speedups in late game: all into research (to reach T5) vs keep them for KvK healing — divided

One guide tells F2P aiming at T5 to put every universal speedup into research and never into healing; 2026 commercial guides say to reserve universal speedups for hospital healing during KvK and to use specialised speedups first. The split follows the goal: tech progress between KvKs vs staying in the fight during KvK.
