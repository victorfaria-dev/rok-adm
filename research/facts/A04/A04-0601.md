# Gem price of the T5 building path if bought: Castle 24→25 = 5,000 books ≈ 50,000 gems; all castle books ≈ 200,450 gems; Lv25 blueprints 32,000 gems (derived)

Buying everything with gems, Castle 24→25 costs about 50,000 gems in Books of Covenant (5,000 books × 10 gems), the whole Castle 1→25 chain about 200,450 gems (20,045 books), and one guide adds 32,000 gems for 16 Lv25 blueprints. At the 100 guaranteed gems/day F2P baseline (A04-0001), the 50,000 gems for Castle 25 alone equal about 500 days of that income, so F2P accounts rely on books from barbarian forts.
