# Heal in small batches with alliance help; keep healing speedups for real fights

Guides agree that alliance help is the cheapest way to cut healing time: heal small batches (1–2k troops or ~2-hour chunks) so helps finish them, avoid emptying the hospital with speedups mid-fight, and do not burn healing speedups outside meaningful fighting before a KvK.
