## F2P gem income — current best estimate
Guaranteed: **100 gems/day** (A04-0001). Everything else unquantified: barbarian gem-drop rate, AP regen, gem-node size/time, Peerless pool share (now biweekly, A10-0005), event calendar (A11). One-time: ~100 (CH16) + 200 (Facebook) + quest/code gems before first KvK. Oasis Bazaar (resources → gems) exists only in SoC/events (C04-0009) — not for early kingdoms.

## Known gaps
- Drop probability of the gem item from barbarians; gems per AP.
- Gem deposit amounts and gathering rate (T0 needed); Jewelry research cost (A02).
- Peerless Scholar gem pool size and typical payout.
- Main/side quest gem totals before CH10 (BlueStacks says most early free gems come from these).
- Expedition medal income/day; Alliance Shop prices for speedups.
- Wheel of Fortune now uses Spin Tickets (C04-0022) — S04's gem-per-spin math (in raw) is obsolete; not carded.
- Reddit r/RiseofKingdoms (HTTP 403) — real F2P gem logs not available.
