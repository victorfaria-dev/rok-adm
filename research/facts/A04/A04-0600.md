# Gems after VIP 10 (late game): Books of Covenant for Castle 25 vs VIP 12/14 sculptures; VIP Shop and Courier only with spare gems

After VIP 10, guides split the free gems between Books of Covenant (Castle 25, needed for Academy 25 and T5) and more VIP levels (VIP 12 = 2 and VIP 14 = 3 legendary sculptures per day), buying during More Than Gems. In the 2022 rkg priority list Wheel of Fortune sits between VIP 12 and VIP 14, while VIP Shop and discounted Courier Station items are only for spare gems.
