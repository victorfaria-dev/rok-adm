# No daily cap on receiving resource assistance/plunder: the 200M cap of 1.0.49 (Aug 2021) was cancelled the next day

Update 1.0.49 announced a daily cap of 200M resources received through Resource Assistance and 200M through plundering per governor; after 73% of governors objected, Lilith removed both limits on 2021-08-04 while keeping the +25% resource-point deposits and +10% base gathering speed.
