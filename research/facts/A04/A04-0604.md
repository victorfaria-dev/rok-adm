# Pre-KvK stockpile: gather continuously, keep resources as items, favour gold once on T5 (numeric targets single-source)

For the gap before KvK2/KvK3/SoC, guides agree to gather (and use farm accounts) continuously, keep reserves as unopened resource items (they cannot be plundered) and, once on T5, prioritise gold. Only one seller guide gives numbers: at least 500M food, 500M wood, 200M stone and 100M gold in item form.
