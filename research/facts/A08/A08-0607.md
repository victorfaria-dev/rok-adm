# Upgrade T4→T5 (or train T5) inside MGE / power events with all speed buffs on; whether upgrading costs more than training directly is disputed

Guides advise converting troops to T5 only during scored events (Mightiest Governor training/power stages, Zenith of Power) with runes, kingdom buffs and titles active, keeping a T4 reserve for field fights. Whether upgrading is cheaper or dearer is disputed: one guide (2022) says T4 cost plus upgrade cost equals training T5 directly, while the wiki's Barracks page says upgrading has a greater overall resource cost than training the higher tier directly. MGE awards 100 points per T5 trained vs 40 per T4, and upgrades earn the point difference.
