# Upgrade T4→T5 (or train T5) inside MGE / power events with all speed buffs on; upgrade costs the same as training directly

Guides advise converting troops to T5 only during scored events (Mightiest Governor training/power stages, Zenith of Power) with runes, kingdom buffs and titles active, keeping a T4 reserve for field fights. One guide notes the total cost of upgrading T4→T5 equals training T5 directly, so upgrading gives event points without extra cost. MGE awards 100 points per T5 trained vs 40 per T4, and upgrades earn the point difference.
