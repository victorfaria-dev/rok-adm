# Packsify: healing 100,000 T5 ≈ 192M food/192M wood/144M stone/144M gold vs T4 ≈ 72M/72M/54M/7.2M

A 2026 high-spender guide (top-up seller) states that healing 100,000 T5 costs about 192M food, 192M wood, 144M stone and 144M gold, versus about 72M food, 72M wood, 54M stone and 7.2M gold for T4, i.e. 20× the gold. Its absolute numbers are 6–9× higher than the riseofkingdomsguides calculator and list all four resources for one unit count, so they are unverified.
