# Hall of Heroes returns about half of dead troops (single 2022 guide; likely outdated)

A 2022 Heroic Anthem guide says that at the end of the KvK half of the troops you lost are returned by mail through the Hall of Heroes. No official percentage was found, and since 1.0.94 the official rate varies with the Lost Kingdom tier, so a flat 50% should not be used for planning.
