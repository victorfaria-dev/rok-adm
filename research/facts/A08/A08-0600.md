# Fate of severely wounded units by battle context (hospital, half dead, all dead)

Severely wounded units go to the hospital when defending your own city, fighting on the open map/resource points, or at sanctums, altars and Lv1 passes; half die when reinforcing an ally city/alliance building or fighting at shrines and Lv2 passes; all die at the Lost Temple, Lv3 passes and when attacking cities or alliance buildings. Wounded that do not fit in the hospital die. Slightly wounded do not use the hospital and are healed instantly on return to the city. Only the city-reinforcement 50% row (T1, 2025) and the hospital-overflow row (2026 guide) have recent confirmation; the sanctum/altar/shrine/pass/Lost Temple rows come only from a 2020 wiki revision.
