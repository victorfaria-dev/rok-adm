# Fate of severely wounded units by battle context (hospital, half dead, all dead)

Severely wounded units go to the hospital when defending your own city, fighting on the open map/resource points, or at sanctums, altars and Lv1 passes; half die when reinforcing an ally city/alliance building or fighting at shrines and Lv2 passes; all die at the Lost Temple, Lv3 passes and when attacking cities or alliance buildings. Wounded that do not fit in the hospital die. Slightly wounded heal on return to the city at no cost.
