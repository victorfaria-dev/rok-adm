# Maximum base hospital capacity: 4 hospitals × 75,000 = 300,000 wounded (Lv25), 260,000 at Lv24, 200,000 at Lv21 (derived)

With all four hospitals at the same level, base wounded capacity is 200,000 at Lv21, 260,000 at Lv24 and 300,000 at Lv25 (Lv25 requires Castle 25 and a Master’s Blueprint). Capacity buffs (research, alliance, items) come on top and were not quantified.
