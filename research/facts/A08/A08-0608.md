# Official healing-cost relief in late seasons: crystal tech, Hall of Heroes refund, Recovery Potions, seasonal strategies, WoF healing speedups

Lilith has addressed T5 healing cost with season-only levers instead of lowering base costs: SoC crystal technologies that reduce T5 healing cost (2021), seasonal strategies such as Frugality (−10% heal resources, capped at 700M per season) and Medical Skills (+15% healing speed, capped at 6,000 min) in King of the Nile (1.0.77), healing speedups in the SoC Wheel of Fortune pool (1.0.92), a Hall of Heroes refund of part of Lost Kingdom healing resources (1.0.89) and Recovery Potions bought with Acclaim that instantly heal severely wounded (pioneer, 1.1.04).
