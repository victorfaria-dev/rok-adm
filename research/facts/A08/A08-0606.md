# Late stages: T4 for open-field fighting, T5 for rallies, garrisons, 1v1 and Ark of Osiris

Guides agree that once T5 exists, accounts without a heavy gold economy should keep T4 for large open-field fights (cheaper heals, better trades) and use T5 where stats decide the outcome: rallies, garrison defence, 1v1 fights and Ark of Osiris (healed for free at the end).
