# Hall of Heroes (official): dead units come back at the end of the season; return rate rises with Lost Kingdom tier; part of healing resources refunded

Official notes describe a Hall of Heroes that holds units lost in the Lost Kingdom and returns them at the end of the Season of Conquest even if the governor is offline (1.0.45, 2021); those troops count for Imperium power (1.0.47); since 1.0.94 (May 2025) Lost Kingdom tier-ups (earned from dead and severely wounded units) increase the Hall of Heroes unit return rate; since 1.0.89 (Dec 2024) it also refunds part of the resources spent healing in the Lost Kingdom. The return percentage is not stated in any official post.
