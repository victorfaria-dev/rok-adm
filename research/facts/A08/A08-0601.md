# Hall of Heroes (official): dead units come back at the end of the season; return rate rises with Lost Kingdom tier; part of healing resources refunded

Official notes say troops in the Hall of Heroes are returned at the end of the Season of Conquest even if the governor is offline (1.0.45, 2021) and count in the power calculation used to designate Imperium Kingdoms (1.0.47); that the Hall holds units lost (dead) in the Lost Kingdom is player knowledge, not official wording; since 1.0.94 (May 2025) Lost Kingdom tier-ups (earned from dead and severely wounded units) increase the Hall of Heroes unit return rate; since 1.0.89 (Dec 2024) it also refunds part of the resources spent healing in the Lost Kingdom. The return percentage is not stated in any official post.
