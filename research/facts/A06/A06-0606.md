# Shelf life: older legendaries are 'before SoC only' investments; don't expect KvK1 favorites to carry SoC

2026 tier lists mark a group of older legendaries as worth investing in only before the kingdom reaches Season 4 / SoC, with no new investment after that. Named examples are Minamoto, Alexander the Great, Mehmed, Saladin, Charles Martel and Richard I. Many role-specific legendaries are tagged 'no priority' for F2P/low spenders. Over-investing in KvK1 favorites expecting them to carry into SoC is called a common mistake.
