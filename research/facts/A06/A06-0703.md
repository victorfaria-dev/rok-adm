# Golden Chests gained Pepin III and Casimir III (1.1.01); Achilles and Hector joined the Legendary Tavern, a Season of Conquest event since 1.0.44

The 1.1.01 notes (November 2025) say Golden Chests and Commander Sculpture Chests now also contain the gathering commanders Pepin III and Casimir III, and that Achilles and Hector were added to the Legendary Tavern. The Legendary Tavern was introduced in 1.0.44 (March 2021) as an event that runs after a kingdom enters Season of Conquest; later notes also use it for Season 2 commanders (1.0.94) and for "subsequent seasons" after Season 2 (1.0.96), so the season in which it first runs is not stated in one place.
