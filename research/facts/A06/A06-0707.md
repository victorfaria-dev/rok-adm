# Wheel of Fortune commander order by kingdom age (rkg, 2022): Richard day 38, Yi Seong-Gye day 94, Genghis Khan day 150 …

A 2022 rkg guide gives an estimated Wheel of Fortune order by kingdom age, alternating a random commander and a fixed one: day 38 Richard, 94 Yi Seong-Gye, 150 Genghis Khan, 206 Alexander, 262 Edward of Woodstock, 318 Takeda Shingen, 374 Guan Yu, 430 Ramesses, 496 Yi Sun-sin, 552 William I, 608 Harald Sigurdsson (random commanders on days 24, 80, 136, 192, 248, 304, 360, 416, 472, 538, 594). The guide says there is no set schedule.
