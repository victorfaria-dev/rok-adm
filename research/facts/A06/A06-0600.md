# Commander pools are season-gated: Season 3 Mightiest Governor/Wheel of Fortune include all commanders

Which legendaries a kingdom can win in the Mightiest Governor (MG) and Wheel of Fortune (WoF) events depends on the kingdom's Lost Kingdom season. Since 1.0.91 (Feb 2025), the MG/WoF reward lists in Season 3 kingdoms include all commanders, so Season of Conquest (SoC) commanders can be won in Season 3. The developers had already described SoC commanders as available in Season 3 in May 2023. The lists still change by patch: 1.0.94 (May 2025) moved four commanders to Season 2 and removed three of them from the SoC Wheel of Fortune list. In 2021 the 'Commander of Choice' in MG/WoF unlocked only after a kingdom finished Light and Darkness (the old Season 3); that is historical context, and no later official text says whether a similar gate still exists.
