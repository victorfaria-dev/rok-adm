# When the Commander Swap / Gathering of Heroes window opens: around the Season 2 → Season 3 transition

The Legendary Commander Swap and Gathering of Heroes window for new kingdoms sits at the end of Season 2 / start of Season 3. Official statements moved over time. The Nov 2024 dev Q&A said 'around the end of Season 2'. The Jan 2025 FAQ said the event starts 3 days before the end of Season 2 and lasts 5 days. Since 1.1.02 (Dec 2025) both are listed as events of Season 3 'Light and Darkness'. Players in Dec 2025 and Aug 2026 report that the swap now comes after KvK2 ends.
