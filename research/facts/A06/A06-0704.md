# Prime Ragnar Lodbrok is a Season of Conquest commander: usable only in SoC, sold only in SoC kingdoms

The developers said in September 2024 that Prime Ragnar Lodbrok can only be used in the Season of Conquest and is not in the Wheel of Fortune or Mightiest Governor events. The 1.0.91 notes (February 2025) add his sculptures to Daily Special Offer bundles only in Season of Conquest kingdoms.
