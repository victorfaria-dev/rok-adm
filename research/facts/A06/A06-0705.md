# Pyrrhus and Pericles (1.0.71): Golden Chests and Expedition Shop (Pericles also Silver Chests); Pyrrhus Daily Special Offers from day 43

The 1.0.71 notes (July 2023, Greek civilization) say Pericles can be acquired through Silver Chests, Golden Chests and the Expedition Shop, and Pyrrhus can be acquired through Golden Chests, the Expedition Shop, Daily Special Offers and Card King, and that Daily Special Offers and Card King are available from day 43 of the kingdom's Preparation Season.
