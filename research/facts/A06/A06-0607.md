# Yi Seong-Gye stays useful into SoC (new archer pairings, Museum relic), unlike most early legendaries

Current sources treat Yi Seong-Gye (YSG) as the rare early legendary that keeps value through SoC. He is no longer top of tier lists, but he remains strong for archers, paired behind newer archer primaries (Qin Shi Huang is cited), and his Museum relic received upgrades. F2P players are told to build him and keep him rather than swap him away.
