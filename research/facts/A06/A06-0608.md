# Museum relics extend older commanders' value — relic bonuses work in SoC; some relics purchasable in Season 3

The Museum gives exhibits and relics that boost specific older commanders; relics can be upgraded and new levels keep being unlocked. Since 1.0.81 (Apr 2024), relic bonuses added from that point on take effect only in Season of Conquest. Since 1.1.03/1.1.04 (early 2026), relics of Mary I, Archimedes, Takeda Shingen and Siyaj K'ak' can be purchased during Season 3. The developers describe the Museum as a way to give older commanders more chances to take the field.
