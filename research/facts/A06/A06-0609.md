# SoC changes legendary acquisition: Legendary Chest with Sovereign Keys; SoC daily chest gives a Sovereign Key

Kingdoms entering SoC for the first time get a Tavern Legendary Chest from day 7 onward. It opens with Sovereign Keys or Gems and recruits 'even more powerful commanders' (1.0.60, Jul 2022). Since 1.1.02 (Dec 2025), the SoC Daily Objective chest for 100 Activity Points contains a Sovereign Key instead of a Golden Key. After the kingdom enters SoC, Silk Road drops can also yield Sovereign Keys (1.0.79).
