# Legendary Commander Swap rules: 2 swaps, each commander once, ticket cost by skill-level gap

The Legendary Commander Swap (1.0.90) swaps the levels, skill levels and stars of two permanently recruited Legendary commanders. Each governor gets 2 swaps, and each commander can be swapped only once. Swaps cannot be undone, except that a one-time Gem 'Revoke' was added in 1.1.02. The Legendary Swap Tickets needed grow with the skill-level gap: expertised↔1111 costs 120, expertised↔5551 costs 2. Commander level and stars do not affect the cost. Tickets cost 100 Gems each (max 120) or come from a US$4.99 bundle of 12 (max 120). Other rules: commanders with unallocated skill points can't be swapped; sculptures already held are kept; immigrating during the event removes eligibility; requires City Hall 8; no swaps after the event ends.
