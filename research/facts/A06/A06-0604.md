# Gathering of Heroes: quest points recruit unowned legendaries (incl. SoC commanders), tiered since 1.1.02

Gathering of Heroes (1.0.90) runs alongside the Legendary Commander Swap. Governors earn points from event quests and spend them to recruit Legendary commanders they do not own. The Swap FAQ names it as the way for Season 2 kingdoms to obtain SoC commanders to swap into. Since 1.1.02 (Dec 2025), recruitable commanders are grouped in tiers: points unlock the next tier, and recruit cost rises with tier, with the same cost for every commander in a tier.
