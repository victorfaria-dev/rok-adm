# King Arthur: free via King's Trials only after the kingdom completes Season 2

Per the 1.0.90 notes (Jan 2025), the legendary King Arthur can be claimed by completing the quests of the King's Trials event. The event is only available in kingdoms that have completed Season 2 of the Lost Kingdom. The 'First Strike' trial of him needs Season 3 or later. The Swap FAQ confirms that a self-recruited King Arthur can be used in the Legendary Commander Swap. No official or player source after Jan 2025 re-confirms King's Trials.
