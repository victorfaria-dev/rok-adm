# Why advice goes stale: new commander generations every ~7 months (stated) and design intent to keep old ones relevant

In Nov 2022 the developers said they would stretch the interval between commander generations to about 7 months, from the 6th-gen cavalry update on, to cut pressure from new releases; MG/WoF schedules stay the same. The statement was made in the context of the China server. In Nov 2024 they said the next generation aims to be more consistent so players can plan ahead, giving new and old commanders 'room to work together'. Prime Ragnar was announced as a special commander outside the generations who does not appear in MG/WoF; this was stated for him, not for all Prime commanders.
