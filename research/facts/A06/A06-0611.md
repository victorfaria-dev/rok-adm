# Why advice goes stale: new commander generations every ~7 months (stated) and design intent to keep old ones relevant

In Nov 2022 the developers said they would stretch the interval between commander generations to about 7 months, from the 6th-gen cavalry update on. They said this to cut pressure from new releases. MG/WoF schedules stay the same. The statement was made in the context of the China server. In Nov 2024 they said the next generation aims to be more consistent so players can plan ahead, giving new and old commanders 'room to work together'. Prime/special commanders (e.g. Prime Ragnar) sit outside the generations and outside MG/WoF.
