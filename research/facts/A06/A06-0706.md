# How Fandom says early and Season 2 legendaries are obtained: gold chests, VIP chests, Wheel of Fortune, "kingdoms over 140/240 days"

The Fandom commander pages give these routes. Gold chests: Cao Cao, Charles Martel (also round 2 of The Mightiest Governor), Julius Caesar (also the first Mightiest Governor), El Cid (also round 3) and Cleopatra VII. VIP chests (VIP 10–14): Hannibal Barca. Wheel of Fortune only: Cyrus the Great, Alexander the Great and Genghis Khan. Yi Seong-Gye and Richard I are not in chests on kingdoms #1032 and later and come from (early) Wheels of Fortune in new kingdoms. "Kingdoms over 140 days" only: Constantine I, Edward of Woodstock, Saladin, Tomyris and Wu Zetian (Wu Zetian "obtained in Lost Kingdom season 2"). "Kingdoms over 240 days": Takeda Shingen.
