# Since 1.0.55: Yi Seong-Gye unlocks in the offseason before Season 1; Saladin, Genghis Khan, Edward of Woodstock and Tomyris in the offseason before Season 2

The 1.0.55 notes (March 2022) tie commander availability to the kingdom's Lost Kingdom season instead of kingdom age. Yi Seong-Gye becomes available when the kingdom enters the offseason before Season 1. Saladin, Genghis Khan, Edward of Woodstock and Tomyris become available when the kingdom enters the offseason before Season 2; before 1.0.55 they unlocked 140 days after kingdom launch.
