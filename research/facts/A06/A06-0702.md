# Archimedes and Mary I (1.0.96): Wheel of Fortune in Season 2, Legendary Tavern in later seasons

The 1.0.96 notes (July 2025) added the engineering commanders Archimedes and Mary I. They can be obtained from the Wheel of Fortune in Season 2 and from the Legendary Tavern in the seasons after it. Since 1.1.03 their Museum relics can be bought during Season 3.
