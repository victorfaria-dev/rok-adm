# Official season lengths: Seasons 1–2 set to 49 days (2020), Heroic Anthem 50 days (2023); return home 2 days after the Lost Kingdom (2024)

Official notes set the first two Lost Kingdom seasons to 49 days each and Eve of the Crusade stages to 2 days (1.0.41, Dec 2020), extended the Heroic Anthem SoC story to 50 days (1.0.76, Nov 2023), shortened the third Eve of the Crusade phase by 12 hours (1.0.91, Feb 2025) and cut the time to return to the home kingdom after the Lost Kingdom from 3 to 2 days (1.0.90, Dec 2024).
