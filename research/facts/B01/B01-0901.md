# Developers (Oct 2024): back-to-back KvKs strain offseason resources; healing refund planned (shipped 1.0.89); shorter KvKs under study

In the October 2024 developer Q&A Lilith acknowledged that constant KvKs are stressful for players without time to gather in the offseason, announced a partial end-of-KvK refund of healing resources (shipped in 1.0.89 via the Hall of Heroes) and said it was looking at ways to shorten the KvK season and its events.
