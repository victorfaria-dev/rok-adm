# Developers (Oct 2024): back-to-back KvKs strain offseason resources; healing refund planned (shipped 1.0.89); shorter KvKs under study

In the October 2024 developer Q&A Lilith acknowledged that constant KvKs are stressful for players without time to gather in the offseason, announced a planned partial end-of-KvK refund of healing resources (a Hall of Heroes refund of part of Lost Kingdom healing resources appeared in 1.0.89) and said it was looking at ways to shorten the KvK season and its events.
