# KvK season sequence and current Season of Conquest stories

Season sequence: Preparation Season → Season 1 (KvK1) → Season 2 → Season 3 → Season of Conquest (rotating stories: Heroic Anthem first, then e.g. Strife of the Eight, Desert Conquest, King of the Nile, Siege of Orléans, Storm of Stratagems, Tides of War, Alliance Invictus, Shifting Gears, Keener Blades, Song of Troy). Season 3 is moving from 'Light and Darkness' to King of All Britain (pioneer since May 2026).
