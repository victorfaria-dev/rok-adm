# Lost Kingdom (KvK1) entry needs City Hall 17; opens after Eve of the Crusade

Entering the Lost Kingdom requires City Hall 17 (single T3, age unknown). The older rule 'the map opens after Eve of the Crusade' is outdated: Eve of the Crusade starts after entering the Lost Kingdom in SoC (T1 1.0.95) and, per 1.1.12 (T3) and a 2026 player report, the first ~6 days of pre-KvK now overlap the opening of the Lost Kingdom in Season 1 too.
