# T5 research gate: Medical Corps 10 + Scutum 10 + Academy 25; T5 tech costs 10M/10M/7.5M/10M and 100 days raw

Each T5 unit tech (e.g. Royal Guard) needs Medical Corps level 10, the tier-4 defence tech of that line (Scutum 10 for infantry) and Academy 25, and costs 10.0M food, 10.0M wood, 7.5M stone and 10.0M gold with a raw research time of 100 days 1 hour. Medical Corps alone (Academy 23–24) takes 231 days 14 hours raw at level 10 (19.2M/19.2M/14.4M/11.5M). Raw times assume no research bonuses.
