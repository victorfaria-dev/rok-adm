# T5 research gate: Medical Corps 10 + line defence tech 10 + Academy 25; T5 tech costs 10M/10M/7.5M/10M and 100 days raw

Each T5 unit tech needs Medical Corps level 10, Academy 25 and the level-10 defence tech of its line (Royal Guard: Scutum; Royal Crossbowman: Pavise; Royal Knight: Plate Armor; Trebuchet: Heavy Frame), and costs 10.0M food, 10.0M wood, 7.5M stone and 10.0M gold with a raw research time of 100 days 1 hour (Trebuchet 150d 1h). The Medical Corps level-10 step alone (needs Academy 24, Combined Arms 10 and Encampment 10) takes 231 days 14 hours raw and 19.2M/19.2M/14.4M/11.5M; levels 1–10 together take about 461 days raw (sum of the wiki table). Raw times assume no research bonuses.
