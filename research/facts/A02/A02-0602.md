# Crystal tech priorities for F2P/low: specialise in one unit type, some crystal economy early, do not max everything

Guides advise F2P/low spenders to be selective in crystal tech: they cannot max every branch, should invest in production (Crystals, Favor) early without maxing it, and put the rest into the military branch of their one main unit type (which the 2025 rework makes possible officially). One 2022 guide also notes that crystal research yields the seasonal currency used in the season shop.
