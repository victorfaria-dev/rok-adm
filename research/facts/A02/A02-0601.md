# Crystal technology: SoC-only research tree paid with Crystals (Crystal Mine/Research Center); history 2021–2025

In Season of Conquest stories each governor builds a Crystal Mine and a Crystal Research Center and researches crystal technologies paid with Crystals (from the mine, barbarian patrols/forts, Kahar, Trial of Kau Karuak, bastion quests). Lilith cut crystal combat buffs in a 2021 redesign, said in 2021 that some crystal techs reduce T5 healing cost, and from 2025 pioneer-tested (1.0.94–1.0.99) and then rolled out to selected stories (1.1.02) a reworked tree in which Crystals can be invested in the preferred unit type without researching unused unit techs, with earlier Unit Capacity and (announced in an April 2025 Q&A) a rune-summon tech. Since 1.1.04 Crystal tech research and Crystal mine production appear as queues in the Queue Management panel.
