# Crystal technology: SoC-only research tree paid with Crystals (Crystal Mine/Research Center); history 2021–2025

In Season of Conquest stories each governor builds a Crystal Mine and a Crystal Research Center and researches crystal technologies paid with Crystals (from the mine, barbarian patrols/forts, Kahar, Trial of Kau Karuak, bastion quests). Lilith cut crystal combat buffs in a 2021 redesign, said in 2021 that some crystal techs reduce T5 healing cost, and from 2025 reworked the tree so Crystals go only into the unit type you use, with earlier Unit Capacity and a rune-summon tech. Crystal tech research has its own queue (1.1.04).
